Injection-Molded Yoyos

Fall 2018

Poster | Final Report

Project: Design and fabricate 50 identical yoyos using CNC machining, injection molding, 3D printing, and thermoforming techniques.
Course: MIT 2.008 (Design and Manufacturing II), which focused on manufacturing processes and systems and their effects on quality, rate, cost, and flexibility. Topics included process physics, design for manufacturing, and equipment control.
Collaborators: Cole Legg, Laura Li, Heather Nelson, Kiera Gavin

Process Overview

Concept Design

Project requirements specified that our design must include at least two injection-molded parts, including assembly using a press fit, and at least one thermoformed part. We decided on a ferris wheel concept, with three injection molded components — a snapring depicting the ferris wheel beams, an outer body, and a set of 8 carriages that would snap individually into the snap ring. Our thermoformed component would be a stand for the assembly to sit upon to complete the ferris wheel look.

CAD Assembly

Key Design Contribution

I personally worked on the CAD/CAM for the snapring injection mold tooling. We wanted the beams to be as thin and spindly as possible for our ferris wheel aesthetic, but still needed to ensure the part could be ejected reliably from the mold. This ultimately led to the addition of 8 gates around the ring to ensure thorough material flow through the beams.
