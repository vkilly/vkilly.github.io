Design and Manufacturing

Spring 2018

Project: Design and fabricate a robot that can perform a variety of competitive tasks in a Willy Wonka inspired game.
Course: MIT 2.007 (Design and Manufacturing I).This course focuses on the creative design process in conjunction with robust and manufacturable mechanism design, with a heavy emphasis placed on active learning.

Strategy

I set out to design a robot that could climb to the top of a very steep hill and collect wooden eggs from a bucket, which was the most difficult task with the most scoring potential. Since that was risky, I also planned to score points by autonomously lifting a foam cylinder "Oompa Loompa" up a plastic chute.

Early on I created a mini test robot to see how difficult it would be to climb the hill ​(spoiler alert: very difficult)

Oompa Loompa Lifter

To lift the Oompa Loompa up the chute, I create a mechanism consisting of a motorized wheel and a tape measure. Spinning the wheel extended the tape measure, which was thin enough to enter the chute from below and easily retractable. Prototype pictured to the left.

Most Critical Module: Egg Collection Mechanism

For collecting the wooden eggs, I ended up designing a roller intake attached to a winch-activated collection basket. My final iteration included rubber bands in the intake to allow for more flexibility when conforming to the irregularly shaped target objects.

Final Robot

Final Competition

The semester-long design and build project ended in a major competition. My robot, dubbed Violet Beauregarde, finished 8th out of almost 150 competitors!​
