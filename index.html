About Me

Hi! I’m Violet Killy—a New York-raised and MIT-educated mechanical engineer and roboticist with a lifelong love of learning and storytelling. My work has taken me from robotics research at MIT to autonomy testbed development in the aerospace sector. Whether I'm building robots, debugging software, mentoring students, or writing about ideas, I'm driven by the same goal: building robust systems and making them intuitive for people of all backgrounds.

I'm currently based in Los Angeles where I work as a Member of Technical Staff at the Aerospace Corporation. When I'm not working, you can usually find me fencing, rock climbing, hiking, baking, or buried in a good book!
