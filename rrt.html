A Comparison of Robotic Path-Planning Algorithms

MIT 6.881 | Fall 2020

Project: Pursue a research project of your choice relevant to robotic manipulation.
Course: MIT 6.881 (Robotic Manipulation). An introduction to fundamental algorithmic approaches for creating robot systems that can autonomously manipulate physical objects in unstructured environments such as homes and warehouses. Topics include perception, planning, and control.
Collaborators: Sean Kent, Jack Bernatchez

Final Paper

Overview

We chose to explore a popular family of path-planning algorithms, Rapidly-exploring Random Trees (RRT), using simulation-based experiments to compare their performance in cluttered environments across metrics of run time and path cost. We also put forth our own algorithm extension.
